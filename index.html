Ingenium

Ingenium is a self-hosted developer workspace with chat, tasks, documents, skills, and optional email integration. The source and operating instructions are maintained in the project repository.

This website provides application information. It does not host the private workspace, offer public mailbox access, or provide a hosted email service.

Google account connection

An operator can connect a Google account to their own Ingenium installation using Google OAuth. The mail integration can synchronize and display messages, send messages and replies, and manage mailbox folders, labels, and messages according to the authorized features.

Google credentials are handled by Google during sign-in. Ingenium receives authorization tokens and stores protected credentials in the operator's installation. The operator controls account ownership, access, storage, and optional AI features.

Data and support

Read the privacy notice for information about Google data, local caches, and optional AI processing, and the terms of use for the scope of this self-hosted software.

Project support and security guidance are available in the repository. Do not post mailbox content, account passwords, tokens, or other secrets in public issues.
