Privacy notice

This notice describes the Ingenium self-hosted software and its optional Google mail integration. The person or organization operating an installation controls its configuration and is responsible for its data handling. This information site does not connect to your Google account.

Google data accessed and used

When you authorize the mail integration, Ingenium can access your Google account identity and the mailbox data covered by the scopes displayed by Google. These can include message headers, senders, recipients, message bodies, attachments, labels, and mailbox synchronization metadata. The current mail integration requests full Gmail access to support reading, sending, and mailbox management, including deletion when an authorized feature requests it.

This data is used to display and synchronize the connected mailbox, search cached mail, compose and send messages, manage messages and labels, and provide the optional productivity features enabled by the operator. Access to an installation and its connected accounts is governed by that installation's account and ownership controls.

Storage and retention

Mail caches and related metadata are stored in the operator's local application database. OAuth tokens and client credentials are encrypted with deployment-managed encryption keys and vault storage. This does not mean that the entire mail cache or every backup is encrypted. The operator controls storage security, cache settings, backups, retention, and who can access the installation.

Removing a connected account clears its local account credentials and associated caches through the application's supported removal flow. Remote mailbox messages are managed separately. Stored backups may retain earlier data until the operator removes or expires those backups.

Sharing and optional AI functions

Mailbox requests are sent to Google to perform the authorized integration. If optional AI functions such as suggested replies or email summaries are enabled or invoked, relevant message content and context may be sent to the operator's configured model provider. Some functions can run automatically according to saved settings. Operators should review those settings and provider terms before enabling processing of sensitive mail.

Ingenium does not use Google mailbox data for advertising or to train shared or general-purpose models. Google data is used for the authorized user-facing functions. Ingenium's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

You can disconnect an account in Ingenium and revoke its Google authorization in Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access; it does not by itself delete local caches or backups. Contact your installation's operator about local data removal and retention. Project information and support are available in the repository; do not post private account data publicly.
